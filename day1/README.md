# FAIO 2026 — Day 1

<p align="center"><b>English</b> · <a href="./README.kz.md">Қазақша</a> · <a href="./README.ru.md">Русский</a></p>

---

Welcome to Day 1 of the olympiad. Today's four problems each hand you a different kind of raw signal — video, audio, high-dimensional vectors, and text — and ask you to pull structure out of it. Solve them in any order; each task is scored independently. For each task you may choose up to 2 submissions to count toward your final score — if you don't choose, your 2 best by public score are taken. Submissions are limited to one per minute per task. Good luck.

- **[A — The Mysterious Shooting Ride](./a-shooting-ride/en.md).** Watch the ride's camera and predict exactly where to aim: your shot must land on the target as it appears a few frames later, after the ride's firing delay.
- **[B — Keyboard Activities](./b-keyboard-activities/en.md).** Listen to a short recording of someone typing and decide what they're doing — writing code or writing prose — from the sound of the keystrokes alone.
- **[C — Dimensionality Reduction](./c-dimensionality-reduction/en.md).** Compress 128-dimensional data down to the smallest representation you can, then reconstruct it with as little distortion as possible.
- **[D — Smart Router](./d-smart-router/en.md).** Score each document by how likely it is to still contain leaked personal data, so the scarce extra-review budget goes to the riskiest cases first.
