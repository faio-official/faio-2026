# FAIO 2026 — Day 2

<p align="center"><b>English</b> · <a href="./README.kz.md">Қазақша</a> · <a href="./README.ru.md">Русский</a></p>

---

Welcome to Day 2. Today's four problems move from pictures and keystrokes to lectures, language models, voices in a room, and web search. As yesterday, solve them in any order; each task is scored independently. For each task you may choose up to 2 submissions to count toward your final score — if you don't choose, your 2 best by public score are taken. Submissions are limited to one per minute per task. Good luck.

- **[E — Lecture Shuffle](./e-lecture-shuffle/en.md).** A lecture was cut into 30-second clips and their order was lost — put the clips of each lesson back into the order they were recorded.
- **F — Who Wrote This?** Given a prompt and an AI-generated answer, identify which of ten anonymous models produced it — a multiclass classification problem.
- **[G — Direction of Arrival](./g-direction-of-arrival/en.md).** From 30 seconds of four-microphone audio, work out both the direction the speaker's voice came from and a transcript of what they said.
- **[H — Search Engine](./h-search-engine/en.md).** Build a sports search engine: for each query, rank the pages of a large corpus so the most relevant results come first.
